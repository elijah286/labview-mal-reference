Trim Whitespace__ogtk.vi

Removes leading and/or trailing whitespace from a string.

Whitespace is defined as any of the following characters (where values are in the format: dec, hex, string):

<b>tab</b> = 9, 0x09, \t

<b>newline</b> = 10, 0x0A, \n

<b>vertical tab</b> = 11, 0x0B, \0B

<b>form feed</b> = 12, 0x0C, \f

<b>carriage return</b> = 13, 0x0D, \r

<b>space</b> = 32, 0x20, \s
