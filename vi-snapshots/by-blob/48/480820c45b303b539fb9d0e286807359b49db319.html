Standard Measurement Plugin.lvlib:Standard Measurement.lvclass:Read Buffered Data.vi

Read one snapshot from each configured Standard Measurement hardware buffer and store latest waveforms. Three hardware roles maximum; each preview waits at most 1 ms, zero retries. Incoming errors bypass reads through the framework accessors; timeout or type conversion errors propagate. No hardware initialization or actor launch.
