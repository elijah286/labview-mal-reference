Encode Section and Key Names__ogtk.vi

Encodes and decodes section and key names.

Escapes the following:

\ (escape code special character escaped as \\)

\00-\1F (non-printable chars)

"[", "]", and "=" (INI syntax elements)

\FE ("þ") and \FF ("ÿ") (legacy encoding)

Legacy encoding encodes brackets, "[" and "]", as special characters "þ" and "ÿ".
