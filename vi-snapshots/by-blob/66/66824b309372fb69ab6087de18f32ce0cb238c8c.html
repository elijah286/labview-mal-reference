Convert Dirs to VI Libraries (proxy)__ogtk.vi

Converts an array of Directories into VI Library (.llb) files.

Note: This VI dynamically calls the LabVIEW subVI "Convert Dirs to VI Libraries.vi" located at <LabVIEW>\project\llbedit.llb (as it is not in the VI search paths).
